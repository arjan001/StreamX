# Privacy Policy

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This policy explains what the {{APP_NAME}} app and website (the "App") do with
your information. The App is published by {{PUBLISHER_NAME}} ("we", "us").

The short version: the App has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it
does not sell or share your data. What you type into it stays on your device.

## 1. Kept on your device only

- The sources you add: server addresses, usernames, passwords, portal and MAC
  details, playlist and guide links.
- Your settings, favourites, watch progress and search history.
- The channel lists and TV guides downloaded from your sources, kept so the App
  works quickly.

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the App, or removing a source in
Settings, deletes it from your device.

## 2. Sent to the services you add

When you add a source, the App connects directly to that provider's servers
with the details you entered, to fetch channel lists and to play streams. That
provider receives your login details, your IP address and what you request.
Their own privacy policy applies. We do not see or receive this traffic.

## 3. Sent to our server

The App talks to our server for three things only:

- **Settings from us ("remote config").** When it starts, the App asks our
  server for current settings, such as a notice to show. The request contains
  the App's version and build number, and our server sees your IP address.
- **Error reports.** If error reporting is switched on, the App sends short
  descriptions of problems, such as a playback failure. Each report includes
  the App version, the device model and system version, the screen size, which
  playback engine was used, whether the device was online, and a random number
  that changes every time the App starts. A report can include the address of
  the server or stream that failed. Before sending, the App removes usernames,
  passwords, device identifiers and other login details from addresses. Our
  server does not store your IP address with reports. Reports are deleted after
  7 days. They are used only to find and fix problems.
- **Live captions.** Only when you switch on live captions, our server issues a
  short-lived access key, and the App sends the sound of what is playing
  directly to our speech-recognition provider, {{CAPTIONS_PROVIDER}}, to turn it
  into subtitles. The audio is not sent to us and is not stored by us.
  {{CAPTIONS_PROVIDER}}'s privacy policy applies to it: {{CAPTIONS_PRIVACY_URL}}.
  On Android, captions need permission to capture the App's own audio. The
  App does not record your microphone for this.

## 4. Other services the App may contact

- **Artwork and programme images.** Channel lists often include links to
  pictures, such as posters and logos, hosted by other companies. To show them,
  your device downloads them from those addresses{{IMAGE_PROXY_SENTENCE}}.
- **Trailers.** If you open a trailer, it plays from YouTube, whose privacy
  policy applies.

## 5. What we do not do

We do not create accounts, show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, build
profiles, or sell or share personal information. We do not know what you watch.

## 6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them.

## 7. Your rights

Almost everything stays on your device, where you control it. For error reports
and caption keys we hold only the minimal technical data described above, for a
short time. You can ask us about it, or ask us to delete it, at
{{PUBLISHER_EMAIL}}. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights
under laws such as the GDPR, and you may complain to your data protection
authority.

## 8. Security

Your details are stored on your device using the protection your device offers.
Connections to our server use HTTPS. Connections to your provider use whatever
their servers support, which may be unencrypted HTTP.

## 9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and show the new
version in the App and on our website.
